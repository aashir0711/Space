ABOUT SPACE

Space is an extremely vast and mysterious universe that contains billions of galaxies, stars, planets, nebulae and cosmic phenomena. Scientists believe the universe is still expanding continuously, and many secrets are yet to be discovered. Our solar system is only a tiny part of the Milky Way galaxy, yet it holds incredible diversity and beauty.

Space exploration has helped humanity understand planets, gravity, black holes, and the possibility of life beyond Earth. Modern telescopes and space missions like NASA and other agencies are constantly revealing new discoveries.
